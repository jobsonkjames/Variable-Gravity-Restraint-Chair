A design challenge to produce a **low-cost restraining and seating system** capable of stabilising occupant posture under variable gravity conditions. Tight budget constraint of **under ₹2,000**, with all components designed for FDM 3D printing.
 
## Overview
 
The design problem combined two requirements:
 
- **Vertical anchoring** to a surface — since variable gravity changes the load between occupant and floor, traditional weight-based stability fails
- **Rotational positioning** of the seat — since postural needs change with gravity orientation
 
The solution addresses both with a two-part mechanism:
 
- A **vacuum suction base** for fastener-free surface anchoring
- A **lever-actuated rotational locking** seat for adjustable posture
 
## Highlights
 
- **Vacuum suction base** for fastener-free anchoring on any flat surface
- **Lever-actuated rotational locking** mechanism for adjustable seat angle
- All structural and mechanism components designed for **FDM 3D printing**
- Full assembly within the **under ₹2,000 budget constraint**
- SolidWorks CAD models produced and prototype fabricated for validation
 
## Design Approach
 
1. Requirement analysis — vertical anchoring and rotational positioning under variable gravity
2. Vacuum fixture sizing for required hold force on flat surfaces
3. Lever-actuated locking mechanism design for accessible rotational positioning
4. SolidWorks CAD of all components with FDM print orientation and overhangs considered
5. Print, assemble, and test under expected load conditions
6. Cost tracking throughout to stay within ₹2,000 budget
